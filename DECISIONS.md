# DECISIONS

Judgment calls made without asking, newest at the bottom.

1. **Playwright browsers.** Disk had 4.5 GB free and the user previously ran out of space for Unity. Chromium tests use the installed Google Chrome (`channel: 'chrome'`) instead of downloading Playwright's Chromium; only Firefox and WebKit are downloaded (~180 MB total). Tests live in `tests/` with their own `package.json`; the game itself still has no build step or install.
2. **Git identity.** Commits use the repo owner's name/email locally plus the `Co-Authored-By` trailer.
3. **Start-screen bug.** Could not reproduce in Chrome, Firefox or WebKit with real clicks/Space/Enter, with pointer lock allowed or denied (18/18 pass). Most likely cause on the user's machine: an exception inside the frame loop. three.js's `setAnimationLoop` stops rescheduling after a throw, which freezes the first frame (help box + controls visible). Fix: every frame is wrapped in try/catch and errors show a banner; startup errors show a full-screen overlay; stale code is prevented by a no-store dev server, `Cache-Control: no-cache` on Netlify and a visible build stamp. The only real failure found was a missing favicon (404).
4. **Witness rule applies to all civilian crimes**, not only gunfire: a cop with line of sight (90 m) reacts at once; a pedestrian with line of sight (45 m) calls after 6 s unless knocked down first. Crimes against police always count. Gang members never call the police.
5. **Performance budget.** Target 60 FPS on Medium at 1280x720, hard floor 50 in the automated test (headless timing is noisy). Fixes: humans are one skinned mesh each (was ~20 meshes), drivable cars share merged geometry (~9 draws, was ~24), bigger instancing cells for props, sky reflection map only rebuilt on large lighting changes (removed periodic frame spikes), CPU-side world geometry freed after GPU upload.
6. **Quality tiers redefined.** 4x MSAA on the half-float target cost ~40% FPS. Low = no post; Medium = bloom + FXAA + 2048 shadows (default); High = bloom + 2x MSAA + 4096 shadows; Ultra = 4x MSAA + ambient occlusion (AO re-renders the scene, so Ultra is for strong GPUs).
7. **Key bindings.** The spec puts the weapon wheel on Q, so cover moved to C (GTA PC uses Q for cover), camera distance to V, radio to N, phone to P, interact (shops, safehouse) to E, enter/exit vehicle stays on F. All rebindable in Settings.
8. **Real vs invented names for shops.** Shops that match a real business type reuse the real place's location (a clothing store, a grocery, the Shell station, a car-repair shop) and keep its real name in "real" brand mode. Anything involving weapons or police uses invented names at unnamed buildings ("Turnpike Sporting Goods", "Midlothian Police Substation") so no real business is shown selling guns. The hospital respawn is the real Fire & EMS Station 5 (a public place).
9. **Water.** Players swim at the surface (slower, weapons holstered, stamina for faster strokes). Vehicles that drive into a pond flood after 2.5 s and are abandoned (no sinking animation). No diving.
10. **Flips.** Vehicles are simulated in 2D, so they only roll over from big side impacts or nearby explosions; R puts a flipped vehicle back on its wheels (instead of GTA's rocking).
11. **Helicopter (stretch) shipped in simple form:** circles the player or last known position at 5 stars, searchlight, a marksman with low accuracy, can be shot down. No flying physics or crashing animation.
12. **Bikes.** Motorcycles and bicycles are player-only (no AI riders). One of each is always parked: a bicycle at the safehouse, a motorcycle at the gas station. Big crashes throw the rider off.
13. **Ambient street race** reuses the side-job race framework from Phase 3 (until then the event simply doesn't trigger).
14. **More performance work** after adding Phase 2 systems: the static world sits under one parent excluded from per-frame matrix updates, effect pools update matrices only when moved, instanced and plain meshes never share a material (avoids per-frame shader re-selection), trees use 4 kinds + one shared far mesh per cell, traffic signals/stop signs are merged (2,000 objects -> 4), 600 m chunks, 3 ground tiers. Draw calls ~1,600 -> ~1,000.
15. **Test isolation.** Gameplay suites freeze the live loop (`__test.freeze`) and drive frames with `__test.run`, so real-time updates can't race the test.
16. **Story cast and sites.** All invented: Dex Carver (player) comes home to help Rosa Quill (tow-shop owner), Tick (Marcus Tillman, hustler) and Harlan Pryce (retired county clerk) stop developer Victor Graves from digging up Mid-Lothian Mines Park. Mission sites (Quill's Tow & Tune, Graves Holdings, the storage lot) are placed at *unnamed* commercial buildings at least 200 m from any school zone, so no real business is cast as a criminal front. Sites and markers are snapped to open, dry ground (a park centroid sat in a pond; one building had no sidewalk within 90 m).
17. **Mission fairness changes found by the bot.** Tail targets wait until you've caught up and take a longer route; chase targets cruise until they notice you, escape only 10 s after you've fallen 350 m behind *once you've been close*, and top out at 20-21 m/s so ordinary cars can catch them. Stealth detection scales with distance (17 m range), guards spawn facing away from the player with a 3 s grace period, and a silent takedown from behind (E) was added; shooting or stunning a guard still raises the alarm.
18. **TestBot "assists" are bugs-by-design, logged.** The bot drives/walks/aims with the real input path. Where it can't do what a human would (sneak behind patrolling guards, keep up in a chase from a parking lot), it logs a teleport or assist so TEST_REPORT lists it, instead of the mission silently "passing". Stealth: after 90 s the bot disables detection for itself. Chase: after 20 s more than 120 m behind it hops onto the target's route.
19. **Fixed during Phase 3 bot runs:** mission fail menu could leave a stale flag that froze the next mission; enemy goons and the bot walked straight into walls (both now steer around with ray probes); ambient gang fights could turn mission guards hostile; jobs that need a vehicle reported "started" when they didn't; race destinations were NaN.
20. **Gamepad in menus.** Menus already handle arrow keys/Enter/Escape, so `ui/padnav.js` polls the pad on its own animation frame (menus are open while the game loop is paused) and dispatches those key events: D-pad/left stick = up/down, A = select, B = back, Start = pause/resume. While a menu or the phone is open the in-game pad actions are suppressed so one press doesn't do two things. Settings sliders are still mouse-only (B closes the panel).
21. **Phase 5 fixes, each with a test:** mission vehicles you were still sitting in when a mission ended kept their "mission" flag and never despawned (44 cars piled up over a playthrough, draw calls 1,000 -> 4,400); the police substation marker sat inside its building, so BUSTED respawns and bounty drop-offs were unreachable (place markers now use the nearest open ground by a road); drop-off points snap to the end of the drivable route; the always-parked bicycle could spawn inside a parked car; night characters were near-black silhouettes (brighter ground bounce at night); duplicate map credit.
22. **Edge-clipped roads.** 13 roads at the map edge are unreachable because their connections lie outside the radius. They stay (they're scenery at the boundary); the reachability test covers interior roads only.
23. **Live bot perf runs** play a mission tour inside the real render loop (`__test.botTour`), so FPS, 1% low, draw calls, heap and entity counts are measured during actual gameplay rather than standing still.
